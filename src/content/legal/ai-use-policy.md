## Responsible Technology & AI Use

**Last Updated:** March 2026

AM Masons Advisory may use modern digital tools, analytics platforms, and artificial intelligence technologies to support research, analysis, and documentation.

All technology use is guided by principles that protect client interests and maintain professional integrity.

## Purpose Of Technology Use

Technology may assist with:

- Research and information gathering
- Document drafting and analysis
- Data organization and synthesis
- Internal knowledge management
- Improving advisory workflow efficiency

Technology is a **supporting tool**, not a substitute for professional judgment.

## Human Oversight

All recommendations, deliverables, and analytical conclusions are reviewed by a human advisor before being shared.

Automated tools are never used as the sole basis for decision-making.

## Protection Of Confidential Information

AM Masons Advisory is committed to safeguarding client data.

Confidential or sensitive information is not shared with external systems in ways that could compromise security.

Reasonable safeguards are applied whenever digital tools support work processes.

## Transparency

Clients may request clarification regarding methods or technologies used in preparing advisory materials.

Transparency is maintained in how analytical tools support advisory work.

## Ethical Use Of Technology

Technology use aligns with professional ethics, including:

- Avoiding misleading outputs
- Maintaining data privacy
- Ensuring human accountability

Technology is used to enhance, not replace, thoughtful professional advice.
